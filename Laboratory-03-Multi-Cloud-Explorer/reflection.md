# Mission Reflection

This laboratory helped me understand the differences and similarities among AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform. Among the three platforms, Google Cloud impressed me the most because of its strong capabilities in artificial intelligence, machine learning, data services, and Kubernetes. However, I also learned that there is no single cloud provider that is always the best for every organization.

AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud have many similarities. All three provide computing, storage, networking, databases, identity management, security, and tools for developing and deploying applications. They also provide global infrastructure that allows organizations to build scalable and highly available applications.

Business requirements strongly influence cloud platform selection. For example, an organization that already uses Windows Server, Microsoft 365, and Microsoft identity services may benefit from Azure. An AI research company may prefer Google Cloud because of its AI and machine learning capabilities, while another organization may choose AWS because of its wide range of services and global infrastructure.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud platform requires more than selecting the most popular provider. I need to consider the client's budget, existing technologies, workload requirements, scalability, availability, security, and future plans before making a recommendation.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio also improved during this mission. I learned how to organize research using Markdown files, create comparison tables, document technical findings, and provide screenshots as evidence. This activity also helped me understand how cloud platforms can be evaluated based on actual business requirements.
